Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026  |  Developer: Solelaya Dev (solelaya.dev@gmail.com)

1. Data We Collect

When you sign in with Google, Quizly: World Flags stores your Firebase Authentication UID, email address, chosen username, avatar selection, quiz statistics, coin balance, draw tickets, referral records, and payout requests to synchronize your progress securely.

2. Approximate Location (Country Level Only)

With your permission, we read coarse country-level location solely to verify your country of residence, enforce regional contest eligibility, and calibrate region-appropriate rewards. We do not collect precise GPS coordinates or sell location history.

3. Device Security, Play Integrity & Fair Play

We inspect active network transport state (VPN detection), use Google Play Integrity checks for payout requests, and generate an app-scoped random device identifier strictly to prevent duplicate referral abuse, emulator farming, and automated tampering.

4. Third-Party Services, Rewarded Advertising & UMP Ad Consent

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for sign-in and encrypted cloud sync, and optional Google AdMob advertisements (Banner, Native, Interstitial, and Rewarded ads with Server-Side Verification) to fund free community prize pools. We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and respect your personalized advertising consent preferences in accordance with GDPR, UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws. You can review or update your ad consent choice anytime inside the app via Profile → Ad Privacy & Consent Options. We do not sell your personal information.

5. In-App & Optional Phone Notifications

Quizly: World Flags provides an in-app Notification Bell inbox for Prize Draw wins, Leaderboard Top 5 placements, Monthly Public Pool earnings breakdowns, payout status updates, and app announcements. On Android 13+, OS push notifications are completely optional and only posted if you grant the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission.

6. Age Eligibility & Sponsor Disclosure

Cashouts and prize redemptions require users to be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). All prize draws and leaderboard pools are sponsored solely by Solelaya Dev. Google LLC is not a sponsor of, nor affiliated with, any draw or prize in this application.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your records only while your account remains active. You may permanently delete your account and all associated data anytime inside the app via Profile → Delete Account & All Data, or by following our Account Deletion Instructions.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, payout questions, or data removal requests, email solelaya.dev@gmail.com.
