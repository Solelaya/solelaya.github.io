Frequently Asked Questions (Rewards & Withdrawals)

Clear explanation of how free daily coin rewards, prize draws, badge tiers, and cashouts operate inside Quizly: World Flags.

Daily Coin Reward System & Gameplay
1. Is Quizly: World Flags free to play? Are any purchases or deposits required?

Yes — 100% free to play. There are no deposits, no entry fees, no in-app purchases, and no gambling or wagering mechanics. Coins, Draw Ticket Tokens, and Leaderboard prizes are earned strictly through trivia skill, daily check-ins, and gameplay missions funded by standard advertising revenue.

2. How do players earn Coins every day, and what is a Coin worth?

Coins convert transparently inside the Cashout Wallet at 100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD and are calibrated by region so rewards remain sustainable long term. Players earn Coins automatically from gameplay through:

  • 60-Second Trivia Modes: Classic Flag Quiz, Reverse Flag Quiz, 60s Time Attack Blitz, and Continent Tour with combo streak multipliers up to 3.0x — round Coins and +1 free Draw Ticket Token are credited automatically at the end of every qualifying round (never locked behind ads).
  • 30-Day Daily Login Streak & 24-Hour Daily Missions: Progressive daily Coin check-ins (Day 1 to Day 30), Daily Trivia Missions (complete 3 rounds, resetting at 00:00 UTC), Free Daily Wheel Spins, Continent Mastery Milestones, and the 5-Heart Finisher Chest.
  • Optional Gameplay-Only Rewarded Ads: Players may optionally watch a rewarded video ad strictly for non-monetary gameplay utilities: +1 Energy Heart + 1 bonus 50:50 Lifeline (0 cashable coins) or a Weekly Streak Shield.
  • Public Monthly Pool & Funded Prize Draws: Qualifying quiz rounds count toward the Monthly Public Pool (20+ rounds/month), and funded Daily ($0.05 / 5,000 Coins), Weekly ($0.40), and Monthly ($2.00) prize draws settle transparently at 00:00 UTC.
3. How do Daily Energy Hearts work?

Players start with 5 Energy Hearts per day (1 Heart per 60s quiz round). At 00:00 UTC every new calendar day, if a player's remaining balance is below 5 (0–4 Hearts), it automatically resets back to 5. Surplus Hearts above 5 are preserved, and players who want extra rounds can optionally watch 1 rewarded ad for +1 Energy Heart and a bonus 50:50 lifeline.

5. Step-by-step: How does the withdrawal process work and how long does it take?
  1. Meet Eligibility: Be at least 18 years old (or legal age of majority), keep device location enabled for country verification (no VPN/proxy), pass Firebase App Check & Google Play Integrity device checks, and meet your Badge tier minimum balance. Withdrawals are never locked behind mandatory ad-watch quotas.
  2. Choose Cash or Crypto (USD): Select a Philippine wallet in the Cash tab (GCash, MariBank, or Maya) or select a USD stablecoin in the Crypto (USD) tab (USDT or USDC on TRC20, BEP20, Polygon, or Solana).
  3. Review Fees & Submit: Enter your account/wallet details and Coin amount. Conversions (100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD in USD or PHP), 0 platform fee (0 PHP for Cash / $0.00 USD for Crypto), and your exact payout amount are shown before confirming.
  4. 1–7 Days Verification & Fulfillment: Accounts may hold 1 active PENDING request at a time with a 24-hour cooldown between requests. Submitted requests undergo a 1–7 days anti-fraud, VPN, and Google Play Integrity verification review. Verified payouts are fulfilled by Solelaya Dev and updated to COMPLETED in Withdrawal History (with an automatic alert in your Notification Bell inbox). If a request is declined (e.g., invalid recipient details), Coins are refunded to your wallet with the exact decline reason unless the account is suspended for policy abuse.
6. How do I know if I won a Prize Draw, Leaderboard reward, or Monthly Pool payout?

Tap the Notification Bell 🔔 in the top-right corner of the Home screen anytime to see your full alert inbox — including Prize Draw wins & ticket numbers, Weekly ($1.00) & Monthly Referral ($2.00) Top 5 Leaderboard notifications, your Monthly Public Pool earnings breakdown, payout status updates, and app announcements.
