Frequently Asked Questions (Rewards & Withdrawals)

Clear explanation of how free daily coin rewards, prize draws, badge tiers, and cashouts operate inside Quizly: World Flags.

Daily Coin Reward System & Gameplay
1. Is Quizly: World Flags free to play? Are any purchases or deposits required?

Yes — 100% free to play. There are no deposits, no entry fees, no in-app purchases, and no gambling or wagering mechanics. Coins, Draw Ticket Tokens, and Leaderboard prizes are earned strictly through trivia skill, daily check-ins, and gameplay missions funded by standard advertising revenue.

2. How do players earn Coins every day, and what is a Coin worth?

Coins convert transparently inside the Cashout Wallet at 100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD and are calibrated by region so rewards remain sustainable long term. Players earn Coins automatically from gameplay through:

  • 7 Trivia Modes Across 3 Groups: Flag Quizzes (Classic, Reverse, 60s Blitz, Continent Tour), Geography & Map Quizzes (2.5D Map Silhouettes + Philippines Pack), and World Capitals with combo streak multipliers up to 3.0x and dynamic +35%/+75% difficulty rewards — round Coins and +1 free Draw Ticket Token are credited automatically at the end of every qualifying round (never locked behind ads).
  • 30-Day Daily Login Streak, Daily Mystery & 24-Hour Missions: Progressive daily Coin check-ins (Day 1 to Day 30), Daily Geography Mystery Challenge, 15-point Continent Mastery Badge bonuses, Daily Trivia Missions (resetting at 00:00 UTC), Free Daily Wheel Spins, and the 5-Heart Finisher Chest.
  • Optional Gameplay-Only Rewarded Ads: Players may optionally watch a rewarded video ad strictly for non-monetary gameplay utilities: +1 Energy Heart + 1 bonus 50:50 Lifeline (0 cashable coins) or a Weekly Streak Shield.
  • Public Monthly Pool, Weekly Leaderboard & Funded Prize Draws: Qualifying quiz rounds count toward the Monthly Public Pool (20+ rounds/month) and Weekly Top 5 Leaderboard qualification (50+ played quizzes/week with 3+ correct answers/round). Funded Daily ($0.05 / 5,000 Coins), Weekly ($0.40), and Monthly ($2.00) prize draws settle transparently at 00:00 UTC using a 70% Most-Tickets + 30% Lucky-Random win rate.
3. How do the Geography Map Silhouette, Philippines Pack & World Capitals quizzes work?

Inside the Android app's grouped Home cards and bottom Play Quiz Hub — plus our live web demo above — players can switch seamlessly between Flags, Geography & Maps, and World Capitals:

  • 2.5D Relief Map Silhouettes & Philippines Pack: Identify countries and Luzon/Visayas/Mindanao landmarks (Mayon, Chocolate Hills, Palawan, Taal, Mt. Apo, Batanes) using smoothed 2.5D shaded landmasses, coastal shelves, and optional landmark pins (hidden on Hard mode).
  • Dynamic Difficulty (+35% / +75%): Answer accurately to climb from Easy to Medium (+35% coins) and Hard (+75% coins), while missed questions gently step difficulty back down.
  • 15-Point Continent Mastery & Study Mode: Track progress across Asia, Europe, Americas, Africa, Oceania, and PH Pack — hitting 15 points unlocks a permanent Mastery Badge + Bonus Coins. Toggle Study Mode anytime to learn untimed with 0 Energy Hearts.
4. How do Daily Energy Hearts work?

Players start with 5 Energy Hearts per day (1 Heart per 60s quiz round). At 00:00 UTC every new calendar day, if a player's remaining balance is below 5 (0–4 Hearts), it automatically resets back to 5. Surplus Hearts above 5 are preserved, and players who want extra rounds can optionally watch 1 rewarded ad for +1 Energy Heart and a bonus 50:50 lifeline.

6. Step-by-step: How does the withdrawal process work and how long does it take?
  1. Meet Eligibility: Be at least 18 years old (or legal age of majority), keep device location enabled for country verification (no VPN/proxy), pass Firebase App Check & Google Play Integrity device checks, and meet your Badge tier minimum balance. Withdrawals are never locked behind mandatory ad-watch quotas.
  2. Choose Cash or Crypto (USD): Select a Philippine wallet in the Cash tab (GCash, MariBank, or Maya) or select a USD stablecoin in the Crypto (USD) tab (USDT or USDC on TRC20, BEP20, Polygon, or Solana).
  3. Review Fees & Submit: Enter your account/wallet details and Coin amount. Conversions (100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD in USD or PHP), 0 platform fee (0 PHP for Cash / $0.00 USD for Crypto), and your exact payout amount are shown before confirming.
  4. 1–7 Days Verification & Fulfillment: Accounts may hold 1 active PENDING request at a time with a 24-hour cooldown between requests. Submitted requests undergo a 1–7 days anti-fraud, VPN, and Google Play Integrity verification review. Verified payouts are fulfilled by Solelaya Dev and updated to COMPLETED in Withdrawal History (with an automatic alert in your Notification Bell inbox). If a request is declined (e.g., invalid recipient details), Coins are refunded to your wallet with the exact decline reason unless the account is suspended for policy abuse.
7. How do I know if I won a Prize Draw, Leaderboard reward, or Monthly Pool payout?

Tap the Notification Bell 🔔 in the top-right corner of the Home screen anytime to see your full alert inbox — including Prize Draw wins & ticket numbers, Weekly ($1.00) & Monthly Referral ($2.00) Top 5 Leaderboard notifications, your Monthly Public Pool earnings breakdown, payout status updates, and app announcements.

8. Why might a withdrawal be declined or held?

To protect honest players and advertiser integrity, our SecurityMonitor checks Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity signals. Using rooted devices, emulators, VPNs/proxies, automated clickers, tampered APKs, or multiple accounts for self-referrals violates Fair Play Rules and blocks or voids withdrawal requests. For payout support or disputes, contact solelaya.dev@gmail.com.

9. What is the Impact Score and how does the Top Sharers leaderboard work?

Your Impact Score is a community sharing counter that increases by +1 each time you share your Quizly: World Flags referral link using Share to Socials, Share via WhatsApp (on mobile devices), or Copy Referral Link (with a 10-second anti-spam cooldown). It is saved locally on your browser and syncs securely to Cloud Firestore when you sign in with Google, ranking the top 5 community ambassadors on the live Top Sharers leaderboard. Impact Score is purely a social recognition score and never pays for ad clicks or requires purchases.
