Frequently Asked Questions (Rewards & Withdrawals)

Clear explanation of how free daily coin rewards, prize draws, badge tiers, and cashouts operate inside Quizly: World Flags.

Daily Coin Reward System & Gameplay
1. Is Quizly: World Flags free to play? Are any purchases or deposits required?

Yes — 100% free to play. There are no deposits, no entry fees, no in-app purchases, and no gambling or wagering mechanics. Coins, Draw Ticket Tokens, and Leaderboard prizes are earned exclusively through trivia skill, daily check-ins, and optional rewarded video ads funded by advertising revenue.

2. How do players earn Coins every day, and what is a Coin worth?

Coins convert transparently inside the Cashout Wallet at 100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD. Players earn Coins daily through:

  • 60-Second Trivia Modes: Classic Flag Quiz, Reverse Flag Quiz, 60s Time Attack Blitz, and Continent Tour with streak multipliers up to 3.0x.
  • Optional Quiz Rewarded Ads: After finishing a round, players can choose to watch a video ad to collect round Coins (+1 free Draw Ticket Token) or claim a 2X Coin Boost.
  • 30-Day Daily Login Streak & Missions: Progressive daily Coin claims (Day 1 to Day 30), Lucky Wheel Spins, Daily Quests, and the 5-Heart Finisher Chest.
  • Public Monthly Pool & Draws: Quiz ads count 1:1 (no daily limit) toward the Monthly Public Pool once you reach 20+ quiz ads in the month, and Daily ($0.25–$0.50), Weekly ($0.25–$2), and Monthly ($0.25–$5) prize draws settle transparently at 00:00 UTC.
3. How do Daily Energy Hearts work?

Players start with 5 Energy Hearts per day (1 Heart per 60s quiz round). Each new calendar day, if a player's remaining balance is below 5 (0–4 Hearts), it automatically resets back to 5. Any surplus Hearts above 5 earned through rewarded ad refills or bonuses are preserved and never reduced by the daily reset.

5. Step-by-step: How does the withdrawal process work and how long does it take?
  1. Meet Eligibility: Be at least 18 years old (or legal age of majority), keep device location enabled for country verification (no VPN/proxy), complete at least 5 ad-verified boosts, and meet your Badge tier minimum balance.
  2. Choose Cash or Crypto (USD): Select a Philippine wallet in the Cash tab (GCash, MariBank, or Maya) or select a USD stablecoin in the Crypto (USD) tab (USDT or USDC on TRC20, BEP20, Polygon, or Solana).
  3. Review Fees & Submit: Enter your account/wallet details and Coin amount. Conversions (100,000 Coins = $1.00 USD in USD or PHP), 0 platform fee (0 PHP for Cash / $0.00 USD for Crypto), and your exact payout amount are shown before confirming.
  4. 1–7 Days Verification & Fulfillment: Accounts may hold 1 active PENDING request at a time with a 24-hour cooldown between requests. Submitted requests undergo a 1–7 days anti-fraud, VPN, Google Play Integrity, and AdMob SSV verification check. Verified payouts are fulfilled by Solelaya Dev and updated to COMPLETED in Withdrawal History (with an automatic alert in your Notification Bell inbox). If a request is declined (e.g., invalid recipient details), Coins are refunded to your wallet with the exact decline reason unless the account is suspended for policy abuse.
6. How do I know if I won a Prize Draw, Leaderboard reward, or Monthly Pool payout?

Tap the Notification Bell 🔔 in the top-right corner of the Home screen anytime to see your full alert inbox — including Prize Draw wins & ticket numbers, Weekly ($6) & Monthly Referral ($8) Top 5 Leaderboard notifications, your exact Monthly Public Pool earnings breakdown (coins earned, USD value, and verified quiz ad watch share), payout status updates, and app announcements.

To protect honest players and advertiser integrity, using VPNs/proxies, emulators, automated clickers, tampered APKs, or multiple accounts for self-referrals violates Fair Play Rules and will void pending rewards. For payout support or disputes, contact solelaya.dev@gmail.com.
