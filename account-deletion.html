Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Quizly: World Flags  |  Developer: Solelaya Dev

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open Quizly: World Flags on your Android device and sign in to your Google account.
  2. Open your Profile screen (tap your avatar on Home).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & All Data.
  4. Confirm Delete Forever. Your Cloud Firestore profile, Firebase Auth user record, and local device data are erased immediately.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request (If You Uninstalled the App)

Send an email from the Google email address linked to your account to solelaya.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Quizly Account Deletion Request". Requests are verified and completed within 7 business days.

Data Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Firebase Auth UID, email address, username, coin wallet, quiz results, raffle tickets, referral logs, and pending/completed cashout request records linked to your account.
  • Retention Exceptions: Aggregated, non-identifiable financial accounting logs required by tax or anti-fraud compliance may be retained for up to 90 days without personal identifiers.
